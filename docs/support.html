AutoPantry Support

Get help

AutoPantry is a local meal-planning app for iPhone and iPad. Most issues can be resolved by restarting the app or resetting your profile from the Profile tab.

Common questions

  • Grocery list empty? Save recipes in Discover or tap + on the List tab.
  • Macros look off? Edit your profile or macro targets in Profile.
  • Custom recipe photo? Allow photo library access when prompted.
  • Start over? Profile → Reset profile & redo onboarding.

Contact

Email caelcummings@gmail.com with your iOS version and a short description of the issue.
