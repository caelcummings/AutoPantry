AutoPantry Support

Get help

AutoPantry plans meals on your device and lets you post recipes to your account. Restart the app if a screen looks stuck.

Common questions

  • Grocery list empty? Save recipes in Discover or tap + on the List tab.
  • Macros look off? Edit your body details or macro targets in Settings.
  • Report or block someone? Use the ••• menu on their profile or recipe.
  • Delete your account? Settings → Delete account. This removes your name, photo, and posted recipes.
  • Start over on this device? Settings → Reset profile & redo onboarding.

Community guidelines

Names, bios, photos, and posted recipes are visible to other people. Do not post harassment, hate, sexual content, spam, or anything you do not have the right to share. Reports are reviewed and content that breaks these guidelines is removed.

Contact

Email caelcummings@gmail.com with your iOS version and a short description of the issue. For a report, include the profile or recipe name.
