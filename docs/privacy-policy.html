AutoPantry Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Overview

AutoPantry helps you plan weekly meals, estimate macros, and build grocery lists. This policy describes what data the app stores and how it is used.

Data stored on your device

  • Body profile (age, sex, height, weight, activity) for macro estimates
  • Nutrition targets and meals-per-week preference
  • Weekly meal plan, favorites, pantry staples, and custom grocery items
  • Custom recipes you create, including optional photos from your camera roll

What we do not do

  • No account is required
  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking
  • We do not upload your profile or recipes to our servers

Photos

If you add a photo to a custom recipe, the image is saved locally on your device. AutoPantry only accesses your photo library when you choose a picture.

Recipe images

Built-in recipe photos may load from the internet when you are online. No personal data is sent with those requests.

Deleting your data

Open Profile → Reset profile & redo onboarding to erase your profile, weekly plan, pantry list, favorites, and custom recipes from this device.
