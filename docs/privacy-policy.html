AutoPantry Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Overview

AutoPantry helps you plan weekly meals and share recipes you choose to post. This policy describes what stays on your device and what is saved with your account.

Data that stays on your device

  • Body profile (age, sex, height, weight, activity) used to estimate calorie and macro targets
  • Nutrition targets and meals-per-week preference
  • Weekly meal plan, favorites, pantry staples, and custom grocery items
  • Recipes you create but do not post

Data saved with your account

An account is required. We store this so other people can see what you choose to share:

  • Email address and account ID, used to sign you in
  • Name, bio, and profile photo
  • Recipes you post, including the photo, ingredients, and instructions
  • Follows, saves, blocks, and reports you submit

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking
  • We do not upload your body stats, macro targets, meal plan, or pantry

Photos

AutoPantry only opens your photo library when you choose a picture. A profile photo or a photo on a posted recipe is uploaded with your account. A photo on a recipe you keep private stays on this device.

Recipe images

Built-in recipe photos may load from the internet when you are online. No personal data is sent with those requests.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account permanently removes your account, name, photo, and posted recipes. Settings → Reset profile & redo onboarding clears the meal plan, pantry, and favorites stored on this device.
